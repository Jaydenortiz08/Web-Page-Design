An education built around your curiosity.

At SUNY New Paltz, academics are about more than completing a list of courses. Your education is an opportunity to ask questions, challenge ideas, collaborate with others, and discover new interests.

With programs spanning the arts, sciences, business, education, humanities, and engineering, students can build an academic path that reflects their goals.

Learning can happen inside the classroom, in studios and laboratories, through research, internships, performances, projects, and experiences throughout the Hudson Valley.
