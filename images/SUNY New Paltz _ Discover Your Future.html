A university with room to grow.

SUNY New Paltz is a public university located in New Paltz, New York, in the heart of the Hudson Valley.

Students can combine a strong academic foundation with opportunities for research, creativity, leadership, and hands-on experiences.

The university's location also places students close to mountains, trails, historic communities, arts, culture, and the natural beauty of the Hudson Valley.
